# whoami

I am a penetration tester and M.Tech (Information Security & Cyber Forensics) candidate who treats offensive security as a discipline: scope it, recon it, exploit it, and — most importantly — prove and report it. My hands-on work follows OWASP Top 10 and the WSTG, from reconnaissance and vulnerability analysis through exploitation and remediation guidance.

What sets my work apart is AI-native security engineering. I design agentic systems — built on Claude and open agent frameworks — that automate real VAPT workflows: recon, exploitation, privilege escalation, and verified report drafting. I use them the way a red-teamer would, and I benchmark whether their findings actually reproduce instead of trusting the output.

Currently a Cybersecurity Intern at SecNinjaz Technologies, an AI-native cybersecurity company in New Delhi, where I apply this exact approach to live web and infrastructure assessments.
